DSE 101: How to Start

1

Find a Licensed Broker

You can't buy shares directly. You must use a broker licensed by the DSE (e.g., Orbit Securities, Vertex Intl, etc).

2

Open a CSD Account

Your broker will help you open a Central Depository System (CSD) account where your shares are safely held electronically.

3

Look for Blue Chips ⭐

If you're new, look for the ⭐ badge in the table below. These are large, stable companies that often pay dividends.
